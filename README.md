Project Overview

This project is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze and monitor sales performance across multiple business areas including regional sales, product performance, shipping insights, marketing impact, taxation, and salesperson contribution.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, and trend analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

Business Problem

Adventure Works required a centralized analytics solution to track overall business performance and identify opportunities for growth.

The business faced challenges in:

Monitoring sales performance across regions
Identifying top-performing products and categories
Understanding the impact of marketing offers and discounts
Tracking shipping and order fulfillment performance
Evaluating salesperson contribution and productivity
Analyzing taxation and revenue trends

This dashboard helps stakeholders gain actionable insights and improve operational and strategic decision-making.
